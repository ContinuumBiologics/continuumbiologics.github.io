What We Do

Six disciplines.
One unifying standard.

01
GMP Readiness & Gap Analysis
Pre-inspection gap assessments benchmarked against current TGA, PIC/S, WHO, and ICH GMP expectations for biologics and biomedical testing facilities — before inspectors find them.
02
Regulatory Strategy & Submissions
Expert guidance on TGA biologics registration pathways, dossier structure, regulatory correspondence, and lifecycle management for biological medicines.
03
Quality System Architecture
Design and transformation of pharmaceutical quality systems — built for regulated biological environments where the standard of evidence is uncompromising.
04
Cell & Gene Therapy Advisory
Specialist advisory for CGT developers and manufacturers navigating viral vector safety, advanced therapy GMP requirements, and Australian regulatory expectations for biological medicines.
05
Biological Testing Laboratory Advisory
GMP and GLP advisory for biological testing laboratories — contract testing organisations, hospital laboratories, and NATA-accredited facilities operating under regulated frameworks.
06
Mock Inspections & Audit Support
Rigorous simulation of TGA GMP inspections — conducted with inspector-level scrutiny — with detailed findings and prioritised corrective action roadmaps.
07
Training & Capability Building
GMP education for quality, regulatory, and operations teams — grounded in current TGA inspector expectations, not outdated training frameworks.
